Transformation

Becoming crystal, becoming light.

Real transformation asks something of you: a willingness to feel the discomfort of growth without shutting it down. This symbol supports you through that shift, easing the adjustment symptoms that come with expansion (the fatigue, the sensitivity, the restlessness) while the deeper work happens underneath. It can also be used to bless and transform anything, food, water, air, back to its highest and most natural state.
