Ambience

Clearing and transforming the space around you.

Your home, your workplace, even a single room can hold onto heaviness that isn't yours to carry. This symbol shifts the atmosphere of a space so it supports what you actually need there, whether that's rest, laughter, welcome or peace. If you've recently moved, or a space just feels off, this is the one asking you to clear what came before and fill it with what you actually want to live inside.
