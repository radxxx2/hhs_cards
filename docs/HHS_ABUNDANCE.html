Abundance

The power to receive.

Abundance is not something you chase, it is something you allow. It comes in doing less, in resting long enough to enjoy what you have already been given. This symbol is asking where you have been taking instead of receiving, or giving without ever letting anything come back to you. Self-nurture opens the door. As you rest, listen, act from that rest and give thanks for what already flows to you, abundance finds its way in.
