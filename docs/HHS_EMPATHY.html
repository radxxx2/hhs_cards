Empathy

Knowing what is yours, and what is not.

You feel things deeply, sometimes so deeply you can't tell if they're yours. This symbol is for the empath who has been carrying someone else's pain, symptoms or emotions without realising it. It clears what isn't yours to hold and sharpens your ability to sense, with real clarity, when something belongs to you and when it belongs to another. Ask: is this mine, or someone else's?
