Accord

Releasing the cords that bind you.

Somewhere, a connection has become a drain. Cords form in every close relationship, and most of them nourish us, but some carry the weight of dominance, old attachment or a bond that no longer serves either person. This symbol is for sensing where you feel heavy, pulled or not quite yourself around someone, and gently severing what needs to go. What's released returns to love. What's meant to stay, stays.
