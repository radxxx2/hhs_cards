Awakening

Coming back to life.

This is for the feeling of watching life through glass, present but not quite touching it. Something (shock, exhaustion, an old trauma) has shifted you out of true alignment, and part of you has been running on survival ever since. Awakening brings you back into your body, realigns you, and reconnects you to the energy that keeps you grounded and alive. You are allowed to feel the breath of life again.
